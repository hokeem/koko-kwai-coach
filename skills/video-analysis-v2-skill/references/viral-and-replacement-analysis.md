# Core Viral Points and Replaceable Parts

## Purpose

Use this reference before writing `core_viral_points` and `replaceable_parts` in `script_table.json`.

These two sections are required final outputs:

- `核心爆点`: why the video works and why audiences may remember, share, imitate, or comment on it
- `可替换部分`: which content elements can be changed while preserving the same theme, conflict, and payoff mechanism

They are first-class analysis fields and should be improved through future TDD cases.

## Core Viral Points

Do not summarize the plot again. Find the reusable content engine.

Check these dimensions:

1. Contrast and irony
   - Does the video rely on a strong mismatch?
   - Example: "平时吃喝玩乐都在" vs "一到关键求助全都消失".

2. Reversal and identity switch
   - Does the audience first misunderstand who is right, wrong, powerful, or vulnerable?
   - Example: the girlfriend initially appears to be pressuring the boyfriend, but the reveal shows she was forgotten at the mall and became the victim.

3. Escalation structure
   - Does the same action repeat in a three-step or progressive pattern?
   - Example: calling several relatives/friends one after another, each refusal making the boast collapse further.

4. Relationship realism
   - Does the joke depend on a familiar relationship dynamic?
   - Examples: couple bickering, spouse bragging, boyfriend acting clueless, relatives disappearing when help is needed.

5. Payoff carrier
   - What delivers the final impact?
   - It may be a line, prop, facial expression, identity reveal, final refusal, or visual cut.

6. Emotional trigger
   - Why would viewers react?
   - Common triggers: recognition, embarrassment, superiority, frustration, surprise, catharsis, envy, desire to imitate.

## Core Viral Point Writing Rules

- Use concrete labels: `极致对比`, `三段式递进`, `身份转换`, `情侣日常代入感`, `结尾明牌`, `关系讽刺`.
- Every point must explain the mechanism, not only name it.
- Tie the point to concrete evidence from the video.
- Prefer 2-4 points. Do not overproduce weak points.
- If the video has a clear joke, twist, contradiction, or emotional reversal, at least one point must explain the payoff.

## Replaceable Parts

The goal is adaptation. Identify variables that can change while keeping the same underlying viral mechanism.

Check these dimensions:

1. Scenario
   - Where else can the same conflict happen?
   - Examples: mall, supermarket, party, gas station, home, office, school, car.

2. Request or task
   - What concrete task triggers the story?
   - Examples: cleaning the yard, moving house, tutoring a child, fixing a computer, borrowing money.

3. Relationship
   - Which relationship can carry the same tension?
   - Examples: spouse, boyfriend/girlfriend, sibling, old classmate, ex-colleague, neighbor.

4. Excuse or obstacle
   - What reason blocks the request or creates the joke?
   - Examples: illness, buying a new car, being busy, phone out of battery, misunderstanding the location.

5. Symbolic action or prop
   - Which object/action visually expresses the conflict?
   - Examples: phone call, shopping bags, car window, two bags of groceries, messy yard, tool box.

6. Ending variant
   - How can the ending change without changing the core mechanism?
   - Example: instead of calling, the forgotten girlfriend appears behind the boyfriend or outside the car window.

## Replaceable Part Writing Rules

- Group replacements by element type, such as `场景`, `求助事项`, `拒绝借口`, `人物关系`, `结局`, `标志动作`.
- Give concrete replacement examples that can become new short-video scripts.
- Preserve the original mechanism. Do not suggest replacements that change the core conflict.
- If the source video has a strong relationship dynamic, replacement suggestions must preserve an equivalent relationship tension.
- Prefer 3-6 groups.

## Examples

### Relationship Help Refusal Sketch

Core viral points:

- `极致的对比与讽刺`: 平时吃喝玩乐都在，一到关键求助就全都消失，形成强烈反差。
- `反转的节奏感`: 用多通电话递进，让丈夫的人脉炫耀被一次次打脸。

Replaceable parts:

- `求助事项`: 修整院子 can become moving house, tutoring a child, fixing a computer, borrowing money.
- `拒绝借口`: can become illness, buying a new car, already having plans, pretending the signal is bad.
- `人物关系`: brothers/relatives can become old classmates, ex-colleagues, good neighbors.

### Forgotten Girlfriend Sketch

Core viral points:

- `身份转换`: 开头让观众以为女友是施压者，反转后发现她被男友忘在商场，是受害者。
- `情侣日常代入感`: 男友理直气壮说自己刚到家，符合“男朋友出门不带脑子”的日常吐槽。

Replaceable parts:

- `场景`: supermarket checkout, party, gas station, restaurant, movie theater.
- `触发动作`: boyfriend drives away after paying, takes a taxi home alone, leaves after refueling.
- `结局`: girlfriend appears behind him, knocks on the car window, sends a photo from the forgotten location.
